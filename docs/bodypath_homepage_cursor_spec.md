# BodyPath Homepage + Product Wedge Spec for Cursor

**Project:** BodyPath  
**Domain:** getBodyPath.com  
**Brand:** BodyPath  
**Spec date:** May 15, 2026  
**Goal:** Build the first homepage and lead-capture workflow for an AI-native consumer health navigation product that monetizes through qualified provider routing, diagnostic test purchasing, and partner referrals.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

BodyPath should launch as a **consumer-facing health navigation brand**, not as a generic “AI doctor.” The first product should help users understand which care path fits their situation, then route them to the right provider category.

The core play:

> **Capture high-intent consumer health demand, structure it through an AI-guided intake, and route qualified users to telehealth providers, lab testing, clinics, and health programs.**

This is the healthcare version of a QuinStreet / NerdWallet / LendingTree model, but modernized for AI, diagnostics, telehealth, and consumer health confusion.

The homepage should make one promise:

> **Understand what your body is trying to tell you — and find the right next step.**

The first version should not diagnose, prescribe, or claim to replace a doctor. It should position BodyPath as a **navigation and decision-support layer**.

## 2. Market Rationale

### 2.1 Why now

The consumer health market is fragmenting across AI chatbots, telehealth clinics, direct-to-consumer labs, GLP-1 providers, menopause clinics, mental health providers, hormone clinics, sleep platforms, nutrition coaching, and longevity/wellness products.

Consumers are increasingly starting their health journey outside the traditional medical system:

- AI health question usage has doubled in one year, with Rock Health reporting that U.S. adults using AI chatbots for health questions rose from 16% to 32%.
- Counsel Health has raised venture funding around an AI-first medical chat experience that can escalate to doctors.
- Hims & Hers, Ro, LifeMD, Midi Health, Quest, and Labcorp are expanding consumer-facing digital health and testing offerings.
- Telehealth brands are increasingly competing for specialized, high-intent patient categories such as GLP-1 weight loss, menopause, testosterone, sleep, and mental health.

This creates a routing opportunity.

### 2.2 Key current market signals

Research references to keep in mind:

1. **AI health adoption:** Rock Health’s 2025 Consumer Adoption Survey found that AI chatbot use for health questions doubled from 16% to 32% in one year.  
   Source: https://rockhealth.com/rock-weekly/one-in-three-consumers-is-using-ai-for-health-questions/

2. **AI doctor / AI primary care competition:** Counsel Health positions around instant medical AI chat plus access to a real doctor for $29.  
   Source: https://www.counselhealth.com/

3. **GLP-1 telehealth market:** Hims & Hers has reached millions of subscribers and is moving between compounded and branded GLP-1 offerings, showing both large demand and regulatory/supplier volatility.  
   Sources: Reuters, Barron’s, Hims earnings coverage.

4. **Women’s health / menopause validation:** Midi Health reached a reported $1B valuation after focusing on perimenopause and menopause care, then expanding into a broader AI-enabled women’s health platform.  
   Source: https://www.joinmidi.com/press-release/series-d-announcement

5. **DTC lab testing is mainstreaming:** QuestHealth sells 150+ consumer-ordered tests. Labcorp OnDemand promotes “Stop wondering. Go test yourself” and offers self-ordered tests.  
   Sources: https://www.questhealth.com/ and https://www.labcorp.com/

6. **Compliance risk is real:** The FTC has warned healthcare lead generators about deceptive and unfair claims, finalized updates to the Health Breach Notification Rule, and taken action against GoodRx, BetterHelp, Cerebral, and others over sensitive health data and advertising practices.  
   Sources: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/12/ftc-staff-sends-warning-letters-healthcare-plan-marketers-lead-generators and https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/ftc-finalizes-changes-health-breach-notification-rule

## 3. Strategic Recommendation

Do **not** launch as:

- “An AI doctor”
- “A telehealth clinic”
- “A supplement brand”
- “A giant health marketplace”
- “A generic lab test catalog”

Launch as:

> **BodyPath: the AI health navigator that helps you understand your symptoms, choose the right labs, and find the right care path.**

The highest-probability wedge is **health-intent routing**.

The homepage should emphasize:

- People feel symptoms but do not know what to do next.
- BodyPath turns symptoms, goals, risk factors, and labs into a clearer next step.
- BodyPath helps users compare care paths such as labs, GLP-1 evaluation, menopause care, sleep care, testosterone care, mental health, nutrition, or a physician visit.
- BodyPath is not a substitute for emergency care or professional diagnosis.

## 4. Initial Product Wedge

### 4.1 Primary wedge

**“What should I do next?” for high-intent health concerns.**

Initial homepage funnel should drive to one CTA:

> **Find My Next Step**

The intake should ask about:

- Main concern
- Symptoms
- Duration
- Goals
- Age range
- Sex assigned at birth / gender identity only if clinically relevant and optional
- Relevant risk factors
- Current labs, if available
- Current medications, optional and clearly marked sensitive
- Urgency / red flags
- Location/state for provider routing
- Email / phone only after value is shown

### 4.2 First care-path categories

Start with these routing categories:

1. **Labs / biomarkers**
   - Fatigue panel
   - Metabolic panel
   - Hormone panel
   - Thyroid panel
   - Vitamin/nutrient panel
   - GLP-1 baseline labs

2. **GLP-1 / metabolic care**
   - Candidate screening
   - Baseline labs
   - Telehealth provider referral
   - Nutrition / muscle-preservation support

3. **Menopause / perimenopause care**
   - Symptom intake
   - Provider referral
   - Lab education where appropriate

4. **Sleep and fatigue**
   - Lab route
   - Sleep program referral
   - Mental health/stress route when appropriate

5. **Testosterone / men’s hormone care**
   - Education
   - Lab route
   - Telehealth referral

6. **Mental health / stress support**
   - Non-emergency guidance
   - Referral to appropriate support
   - Clear crisis disclaimers when needed

7. **Nutrition coaching / lifestyle program**
   - Add-on route after GLP-1, metabolic, fatigue, or lab results

### 4.3 First demo workflow recommendation

Build the homepage around a single polished demo intake:

> **“I’m tired all the time. What should I check?”**

Why this is the best first wedge:

- Broad, evergreen demand
- Emotionally resonant
- Not tied to one medication fad
- Natural lab-testing path
- Natural telehealth route if red flags or abnormal labs
- Works for SEO/AEO
- Can expand into thyroid, anemia, sleep, stress, hormones, menopause, testosterone, metabolic health

## 5. Monetization Model

### 5.1 Immediate monetization

1. **Diagnostic test margin**
   - Recommend lab panels through PopularTests or lab partners.
   - Revenue from test margin, bundled panels, or physician-review fees.

2. **Qualified provider lead routing**
   - Route users to providers based on concern, state, insurance/cash preference, and eligibility.
   - Monetize through CPA, referral fees, appointment-booking fees, subscription revenue share, or affiliate economics.

3. **Sponsored but clearly labeled care options**
   - Later-stage only.
   - Must be transparent and medically responsible.
   - Do not disguise sponsored providers as unbiased clinical recommendations.

### 5.2 Longer-term monetization

1. **BodyPath Plus subscription**
   - Upload labs
   - Track biomarkers over time
   - AI explanations
   - Personalized “next best action” plan
   - Re-test reminders
   - Provider matching

2. **Provider SaaS / marketplace dashboard**
   - Partner providers see qualified demand by category.
   - Lead details are privacy-scoped.
   - Partners can accept/reject leads.
   - BodyPath scores lead quality and routing fit.

3. **Employer / benefits navigation**
   - Later-stage opportunity.
   - AI navigation layer for employees trying to understand care options.

### 5.3 Business model principle

BodyPath should make money when it helps the consumer take an appropriate next step — not when it scares them.

## 7. Homepage Information Architecture

Build a single-page homepage with the following sections.

### Section 1: Hero

Purpose: Make the promise instantly clear.

This is done.

### Section 2: The problem

Headline:

> Health answers are everywhere. Clear next steps are harder to find.

Body:

Consumers are surrounded by AI answers, TikTok advice, telehealth ads, lab tests, supplements, and specialist clinics. BodyPath helps turn confusion into an organized plan.

Pain points:

- “I don’t know which symptoms matter.”
- “I don’t know what labs to ask for.”
- “I don’t know whether this is metabolic, hormonal, sleep-related, or stress-related.”
- “I don’t know which provider type is right.”

### Section 3: How it works

Headline:

> One intake. Multiple possible paths.

Steps:

1. **Share what’s going on**  
   Symptoms, goals, risk factors, medications, and existing labs if you have them.

2. **Get an organized signal map**  
   BodyPath groups your information into relevant themes like metabolic, hormone, thyroid, sleep, stress, and nutrition.

3. **See possible next steps**  
   Learn which labs, questions, provider types, or care programs may fit your situation.

4. **Choose a care path**  
   Compare lab testing, telehealth providers, specialty clinics, coaching, or primary-care follow-up.

### Section 4: Care paths

Headline:

> Built for the health decisions people are making right now.

Cards:

1. **GLP-1 readiness**  
   Understand baseline labs, eligibility questions, side-effect planning, and provider options.

2. **Fatigue and brain fog**  
   Explore common lab categories and care paths related to thyroid, anemia, vitamin levels, sleep, stress, and metabolic health.

3. **Perimenopause and menopause**  
   Organize symptoms and find appropriate women’s health providers.

4. **Metabolic health**  
   Understand glucose, insulin resistance, lipids, liver markers, and weight-related care options.

5. **Hormones and testosterone**  
   Learn what to check before starting a hormone conversation.

6. **Sleep and stress**  
   Decide whether to explore sleep care, mental health support, labs, or lifestyle programs.

### Section 5: Why BodyPath is different

Headline:

> Not another chatbot. A health decision layer.

Bullets:

- Focused on next steps, not endless answers.
- Designed around labs, provider routing, and care decisions.
- Built to help users prepare for better conversations with clinicians.
- Transparent about what AI can and cannot do.
- Designed with privacy, data minimization, and health advertising rules in mind.

### Section 6: Provider routing explanation

Headline:

> Find the right kind of help faster.

Body:

Depending on the user’s situation, BodyPath may suggest different care paths, such as lab testing, a GLP-1 provider, menopause specialist, endocrinologist, sleep program, mental health provider, nutrition coach, testosterone clinic, or primary care.

Important phrasing:

- Use “may suggest” and “care path” rather than “diagnose” or “prescribe.”
- Always include “for informational purposes only.”
- Include emergency disclaimers for severe or urgent symptoms.

- BodyPath does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace a licensed clinician.
- Recommendations are educational and should be reviewed with a qualified health professional.

Multi-select with free-text:

- Brain fog
- Weight change
- Sleep problems
- Mood changes
- Hair loss
- Hot flashes/night sweats
- Low libido
- Anxiety/stress
- Digestive issues
- Muscle weakness
- Other

### Step 3: Duration and severity

- Less than 2 weeks
- 2–8 weeks
- 2–6 months
- More than 6 months
- Severity 1–10

### Step 4: Red flags

Ask carefully:

- Chest pain
- Difficulty breathing
- Fainting
- Severe confusion
- Suicidal thoughts
- Sudden weakness/numbness

### Step 5: Goals

- Understand what labs to get
- Find a telehealth provider
- Prepare for a doctor visit
- Compare care options
- Upload lab results
- Track biomarkers over time

### Step 6: Optional labs

- I do not have recent labs

### Step 7: Location / state

Required for provider routing.

## 13. Analytics Events

Use PostHog or GA4 with strict health-data minimization.

Do not send raw symptoms, medication names, lab values, or free-text health details to advertising platforms.

Track generic events only:

```ts
body_signal_home_viewed
body_signal_cta_clicked
intake_started
intake_step_completed
red_flag_triggered
signal_map_viewed
email_captured
care_path_selected
partner_option_viewed
partner_clicked
```

Safe event properties:

```ts
{
  step_number: 1,
  care_path_category: "labs", // category only, no raw PHI
  has_recent_labs: true,
  source: "organic",
  utm_source: "google"
}
```

Avoid sending:

- Specific symptoms in event props
- Specific conditions
- Medication names
- Lab values
- Free-text responses
- Full IP address where unnecessary
- Data to Meta/Google ad pixels without legal review

## 14. Compliance and Safety Guardrails

### 14.1 Product language rules

Do not say:

- “Diagnose your condition”
- “Find out what disease you have”
- “Get treated instantly” unless actually provided by a licensed partner
- “Guaranteed results”
- “Best provider” unless objectively substantiated
- “Doctor-approved” unless documented

Use:

- “Educational guidance”
- “Possible care paths”
- “Questions to ask a clinician”
- “Labs to discuss or consider”
- “Provider options”
- “Not a diagnosis”

### 14.2 Disclaimers

Footer disclaimer:

> BodyPath provides educational health navigation and care-path information. BodyPath does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, emergency care, or prescriptions. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice. If you may be experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or seek emergency care immediately.

CTA disclaimer:

> Not emergency care. Not a diagnosis.

Partner disclosure:

> BodyPath may receive compensation when users choose certain partner services. Sponsored or partner options are labeled. Compensation does not determine whether a care path is medically appropriate.

### 14.3 Privacy posture

Because BodyPath may collect health-related information, build from day one as if it will be scrutinized.

Minimum requirements:

- No ad pixels on intake pages until reviewed.
- Do not transmit PHI/sensitive health data to Meta, Google Ads, TikTok, or analytics tools.
- Keep raw health intake data in your own database.
- Hash or pseudonymize identifiers where possible.
- Show consent language before collecting contact info.
- Create Privacy Policy, Terms, Partner Disclosure, and Health Disclaimer pages.
- Assume FTC Health Breach Notification Rule may apply if operating as a health app/PHR-related entity.
- Get healthcare/privacy counsel before launching paid ads or partner routing at scale.
